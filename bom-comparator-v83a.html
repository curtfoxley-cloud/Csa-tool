ⓘ
The percentage of active EBOM items accounted for in the MBOM via any reconciliation path — direct PN+NH match (Pass 1), EBOM PN acting as a Used On parent (Pass 2), or a reconciled indenture ancestor (Pass 3). The broader planning-intent coverage rate. The gap between this and the strict direct % represents legitimate manufacturing restructuring (kit grouping, phantom resolution, reparenting).

The gap between this metric and 100% is composed of:
• Missing in MBOM — items that failed every path
• EBOM-MBOM Part Qty Mismatch — Pass 1 identity hit but quantity short
• EBOM-MBOM SUB/AR Items Not Used — SUB/AR items that failed every path (acceptable absence by design)
See the corresponding KPI cards below for breakdown. A small residual may also occupy gap slots in edge-case datasets (cancelled MBOM WOs, A/B collectors).
Dev / Logic
Uses Set unions to avoid double-counting: union(direct, usedOn, parent).
direct: ms === 'ok' || 'flex_ok'. usedOn: msUsedOn === 'pn_as_usedon'. parent: ms === 'covered'.
Population: rFunnelScope (active scope minus A/B collectors). Denominator: funnelScopeTotal.
Gap = funnelScopeTotal − (numerator Set size) decomposes as: Missing in MBOM (ms === 'missing') + EBOM-MBOM Qty Mismatch (ms === 'qty_mismatch') + EBOM-MBOM SUB/AR Not Used (ms === 'flex_missing'). All collectors and PT-excluded items are removed structurally by the scope filter; cancelled MBOM WOs do not appear as 'wo_cancelled' rows because the Pass 2 cancelled gate produces 'n/a' on the used-on leg rather than a status.
Same methodology as the Traceability Funnel MBOM panel.

EBOM-MBOM Part Parentage Delta
Informational — restructuring is normal
ⓘ
The count of distinct highest-level EBOM items whose entire indenture subtree is missing from the MBOM. For each item with ms='missing', the tool walks up the EBOM next-higher chain until it finds either a reconciled ancestor or the top of the tree — the highest missing item in that chain is the "root" (apex). Resolving a root assembly first is the most efficient path to closing multiple missing items at once, because once the root lands in the MBOM, its descendants may reconcile via Pass 3 (ancestor indenture walk) without requiring individual line-item fixes.

A root with zero descendants is a standalone missing detail part. A root with many descendants represents an entire unplanned assembly.
Dev / Logic
Computed in computeResultsMeta() after reconciliation.
apexMissingMbom: Map<rowKey "pn||nh", apexPn> — for each result row where ms==='missing', walk up NH chain via pnToResult; keep climbing while ancestor.ms==='missing'; last missing ancestor = apex.
KPI count = new Set(apexMissingMbom.values()).size (distinct apex PNs).
.size = total missing rows (matches Missing in MBOM KPI exactly).
Walk depth capped at 20 to guard against malformed cycles.
Self is apex when no missing ancestor exists above it.
Does not alter ms, overall, or any reconciliation status.

EBOM Traceability Funnel — Plain English Guide
What is this chart?

This chart shows how many EBOM line items we can account for in the MBOM (manufacturing plan) and ABOM (as-built record), and which reconciliation mechanism found them. Each bar is cumulative — later stages include everything from earlier stages. The goal is to see how completely design intent flowed through to the build.

Reference bars (top, grey)

Total EBOM — every row in the EBOM file, including collectors, references, and bulk material.
Active Scope — the denominator for all metrics. Excludes Part Type = COLLECTOR, REFERENCE, BULK MATL, and any REMOVAL items (items whose description contains "REMOVAL" without an INSTALL override).

Coverage ladder (green bars)

Pass 1 · Direct — EBOM items whose exact Part Number + Next Higher combination appears in the MBOM/ABOM as a direct line item, with quantity satisfied and (for ABOM) a closed work order. This is the strictest, most audit-defensible reconciliation path.

Pass 2 · Used-On — cumulative: Pass 1 items plus any EBOM items whose Part Number appears as a Used-On (parent assembly) value in the MBOM/ABOM. The EBOM item is acting as a parent structure, not a child component. For ABOM, the associated work order must be closed.

Pass 3 · Ancestor — cumulative: all prior items plus any EBOM items covered because a direct-pool ancestor in the indenture tree was reconciled. Captures kit-grouped and phantom-resolved items where the manufacturing plan tracks the parent assembly rather than the individual detail part.

Delta labels (+N parts, +X%)

Each stage shows how many new items it contributed — items not already counted by a prior stage. This tells you which mechanism is doing the most work. A large Used-On delta means many EBOM items are being used as parent assemblies. A tiny Ancestor delta means the indenture structure is well-represented at the direct level.

Why funnel numbers differ from the KPI card counts

The funnel's Pass 2 and Pass 3 deltas show items first reconciled at that pass — items already credited at an earlier pass are not re-counted. The KPI cards for Used-On and Parent Indenture use a different semantic: they show items with any evidence on that leg, regardless of whether the item also has evidence on another leg. An item with both a Pass 1 direct hit and a Pass 2 Used-On hit is counted on the Direct card AND on the Used-On card (two independent per-leg facts), but only at the Direct stage in the funnel.

Both views are correct — they answer different questions. The funnel asks "how much did each successive pass add?" The KPI cards ask "how many items have this kind of evidence?" The headline percentages on the section cards use a Set-union (de-duplicated) calculation identical to the funnel's overall coverage, so the headline % and the funnel's overall % always tie, even when the individual card counts and funnel stage deltas don't.

Residual Risk (bottom block) and Reference Bars

The residual stacked bar decomposes items that the coverage ladder did not capture (failed all three passes). Segmented by reason:

🟥 Missing — not found anywhere in the MBOM/ABOM at any WO status.

🟧 WO Not Closed (ABOM only) — item is present in the ABOM but only via open work orders (direct pool or used-on pool). Build is pending.

🟪 Proc Removed (ABOM only) — Pass 1 hit, but every record for the key is a removal/swap proc code. Item is traceably absent rather than untraceably missing.

🟨 Qty Mismatch (residual portion) — Pass 1 identity hit, qty short, no other pass saved it.

⬛ SUB/AR — Part Qty Text = SUB (substitute) or AR (as-required). Absence is by design; not defects.

▒ Other — edge cases that don't fit the categories above. Not click-filterable. Usually zero or very small.

Reference bars (below the residual block)

Reference bars show the full per-leg KPI populations from the section cards, for comparison alongside the residual decomposition:

🟨 Part Qty Mismatch — total qty-short items. Includes both residual-portion (in the residual bar above) and items that other passes saved.

🟧 Part WO Not Closed (ABOM only) — total open-WO items per the KPI. May exceed the residual segment count when some open-WO items have additional pass evidence.

Reference bar counts tie to the corresponding KPI cards exactly. Clicking either bar filters the summary table to the full KPI population; clicking the corresponding residual segment filters to just the residual-portion.

Parentage Delta — not shown on the funnel

Parentage delta is an observational flag (the PN exists in the target BOM under a different parent than EBOM specifies), not a coverage gap. It co-occurs freely with any cascade outcome — a flagged item may also be embodied, in-progress, or missing. Because it's not a residual risk and not a coverage gap, it's intentionally absent from the funnel. The Parentage Delta KPI cards in the section view show this population.

Between-panel delta badges (Δ Direct, Δ Final)

Shows how far MBOM reconciliation leads ABOM reconciliation, in percentage points. Neutral grey = MBOM leads ABOM (normal for an in-progress build — planning should be ahead of actuals). Red = ABOM exceeds MBOM, which is unusual and warrants investigation. Hover for exact per-panel percentages.

Click interactions

Click any stage bar or residual segment to filter the summary table to the items first captured at that stage — items not found by any earlier pass. Click the same stage again to clear the filter.

The "Show Coverage Curve" button overlays a line connecting the right edges of each coverage bar. A steep early slope followed by flattening means Pass 1 is doing most of the work. A gradual slope throughout means you're relying heavily on later passes — consider investigating why direct reconciliation is low.
